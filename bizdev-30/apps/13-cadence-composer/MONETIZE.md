# Cadence Composer — Monetization Playbook

## Who buys it

Founders doing their own sales, first SDR hires, fractional BD consultants, and agency owners who lose deals to silence — they know follow-up wins, but their "sequence" lives in sticky notes and guilt. Sequencer SaaS (Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo) costs $50–130/seat/month and is overkill when what they actually need is to *design* the cadence and the words before any tool sends anything.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Design your follow-up sequence like a score — before you pay $100/month to automate a bad one."
2. "The cadence whiteboard: email, call, LinkedIn and SMS touches on one timeline, with the actual words written."
3. "Stop improvising follow-up. Compose it once, run it forever, exit the moment they reply."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39.** It replaces a planning spreadsheet, not a sequencer, and it's a single-file tool with zero hosting cost to you — impulse-buy territory for anyone whose next deal is worth 100x the price.
- Optional tiers: **$19** single license · **$49** "Cadence Kit" bundle (app + 10 pre-written cadence templates as importable JSON) · **$99** team license (up to 5 users) with a 30-minute setup call.
- The template pack is the real margin: JSON cadences cost you nothing to duplicate and are the first thing buyers ask for.

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest to launch, discover-tab traffic, pay-what-you-want option for launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout and EU VAT handled if you sell into Europe.
- **Payhip** — zero monthly fee, good coupon tooling for community discounts.
- **Ko-fi Shop** — works well if you already post BD content and have followers there.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo, take 3 crisp screenshots: the Rhythm View staff, an open step editor, and the Copilot panel (light theme, 1280px wide).
2. Record a 30-second GIF: Load demo → click a note on the staff → edit a touch → Copy Markdown.
3. Write the landing page around one line: "The fortune is in the follow-up — this is where you compose it." Include the 3 positioning pitches as section headers.
4. Ship with 2 bonus cadence JSONs (e.g. "Podcast guest pitch," "Conference follow-up") so buyers get an instant import moment.
5. Post the Rhythm View screenshot + one contrarian take ("Your sequencer isn't the problem; your cadence is") on LinkedIn and in r/sales, Indie Hackers, and one SDR Slack/Discord.
6. Offer 10 launch copies at half price in exchange for testimonials with role titles.
7. Add a footer link in the app's exported Markdown ("Composed with Cadence Composer") only if you're comfortable — it's free distribution on every shared doc.

## Upsell paths

- **Cadence template packs** ($19–29): niche editions — agencies, recruiters, freelancers, SaaS founders — sold as importable JSON libraries.
- **Setup + rewrite service** ($150–400): one call where you compose their first two cadences with them, using the Copilot prompts live.
- **Niche editions**: rebrand with adjusted demo data and copy ("Recruiter Cadence Composer") — same file, new market.
- **Bundle** with sibling BizDev-30 apps (Cold Email Forge, Objection Dojo) as a "Pipeline Starter Kit" at $79–99.
