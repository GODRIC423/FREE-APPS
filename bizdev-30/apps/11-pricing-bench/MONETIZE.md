# Pricing Bench — Monetization Playbook

## Who buys it

Solo consultants, freelancers, small agencies, and productized-service founders doing $5k–$50k/month who price by copying competitors and discounting under pressure. Their pain: every proposal is a fresh negotiation, margins are a mystery until tax time, and they suspect — correctly — that they are underpriced but have no instrument to prove it or fix it.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "The workshop where you engineer your prices instead of guessing them."
2. "Good/better/best packaging, true-margin gauges, and a price test log — in one offline HTML file you own forever."
3. "Stop discounting on instinct. Measure twice, quote once."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $29–$49.** It replaces a $200+ pricing-course worksheet stack with a working instrument; buyers at this price don't need a trial. $39 is a solid opener; test $49 with the Claude Copilot angle ("your $20 Claude plan becomes a pricing consultant").
- Optional tiers: **$29 Solo** (app + guide) / **$59 Shop** (app + GUIDE + a filled example bench for 3 service types + lifetime updates via re-download).
- Bundle: sell alongside a proposal or niche-selection app for $79 as a "Get paid properly" pack.

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — best discovery for indie tools; pay-what-you-want launch works well here.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, EU VAT handled, no monthly fee.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero-fee alternatives; Ko-fi suits an audience you already have.
- Itch.io also accepts tools and takes a configurable cut, useful for a PWYW experiment.

## Launch checklist

1. Screenshot set: the loaded demo bench (ruler + three tiers), a margin gauge close-up in the green, the test log with the "everyone says yes = underpriced" plate, and the Copilot rail.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF/video: Load demo → drag a price up → gauge swings → copy the stress-test prompt into claude.ai.
3. Landing copy: lead with the pain ("You haven't raised prices in 18 months"), then the three stations (bench, gauges, test log), then "one file, offline, yours".
4. Post the build story on LinkedIn and X: before/after of a real (anonymized) pricing ladder fixed with the bench check rules.
5. Submit to r/freelance, r/consulting, Indie Hackers, and freelancer newsletters (many accept tool submissions free).
6. Publish one free artifact from the app — e.g. a Markdown "pricing sheet" example — as content marketing; the app is the obvious upgrade.
7. Collect 3 testimonials from beta users (trade free copies), add them before any paid promotion.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup session ($150–$300):** 60 minutes on a call building the buyer's actual ladder in the app with them.
- **Template packs ($19):** pre-filled benches for niches — web design retainers, fractional CMO, bookkeeping, dev shops.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned variants ("Agency Pricing Bench", "Coach Pricing Bench") with tuned demo data and Copilot prompts.
- **Quarterly pricing review:** productized service where they send you their exported JSON and you send back a stress-test report.
