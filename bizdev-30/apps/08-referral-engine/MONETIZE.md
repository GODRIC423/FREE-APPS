# Monetizing Referral Engine

## Who buys it

Freelancers, consultants, and small agency owners (1–15 people) whose best clients came from word of mouth but who have never systematized it. Their pain: referrals feel like luck, not a channel — they know their happiest clients would gladly send business their way, but there's no incentive designed, no list of who to ask, no script ready when the moment is right, and no way to see whether last quarter's asks actually turned into revenue. This gives them a designed program, a working weekly ritual, and a funnel that proves (or disproves) it's working.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Referrals aren't luck — they're a system. Design the incentive once, work the queue every week."
2. "You already have ten people who'd refer you tomorrow if you asked well. This is where you find them, script the ask, and track what it's worth."
3. "The referral program most service businesses never build: an incentive that doesn't feel transactional, a roster sorted by relationship strength, and a funnel that tells you if it's working."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$35.** A single-file tool with no hosting cost and no subscription burden; $27 sits comfortably as an impulse buy for a tool that can source one new client per quarter.
- Optional tiers: **$19** app only · **$35** app + a "Referral Program Launch Kit" (a one-page checklist for announcing the program to your existing client base) · **$69** five-seat studio/agency license.
- Bundle candidate: pair with a testimonial or case-study app from the same suite at $49 ("Prove the work, then let it refer itself").

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest start, built-in discovery, pay-what-you-want option for a launch-week push.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, handles EU VAT if that matters for your audience.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you already have an audience there.
- Your own landing page later, once checkout infrastructure already exists elsewhere.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo and take 4–5 crisp screenshots: the program card, the roster with strength glyphs, a queued ask with its drafted script, the funnel chart, and the Copilot rail.
2. Record a 30–45 second screen capture: pick an incentive → queue an ask → copy the script → move a referral to Won on the funnel.
3. Write landing copy around the pain ("referrals feel like luck"), not the feature list; test the three positioning lines above as headline variants.
4. Publish on Gumroad with the demo GIF as the cover image; price at $27 with a $19 launch-week discount.
5. Post a build-in-public thread showing a real referral program being designed and the funnel filling in over a week.
6. Submit to relevant communities and directories: r/freelance, r/consulting, Indie Hackers, agency-owner Slack/Discord groups, and small-business tool roundups.
7. Offer the first 10 buyers a free 15-minute "design your incentive" call in exchange for a testimonial (which doubles as content for the Testimonial Harvester app, if bundled).

## Upsell paths

- **Done-with-you setup:** a paid session where you help the buyer design their incentive and draft their first week of asks live ($100–$300).
- **Template packs:** channel-specific script libraries per industry (agencies, consultants, coaches, contractors) sold at $9–$19 each.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned variants ("Referral Engine for Coaches", "for Agencies") with tailored demo data, incentive presets, and ask-moment defaults.

## License note

Buyers get the single self-contained `index.html` (all code, styles, and fonts inlined) plus the `src/` source for customization. Suggest a single-user / single-business license: the buyer may use and modify it for their own referral program or resell services built around it, but may not resell the tool itself or redistribute the source as a competing product. Team or agency use across multiple client accounts should be priced as a small multi-seat add-on rather than bundled into the base license.
