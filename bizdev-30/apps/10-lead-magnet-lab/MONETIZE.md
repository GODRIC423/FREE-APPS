# Lead Magnet Lab — How to Sell It

## Who buys it

Solo consultants, fractional executives, boutique agencies, and B2B founders doing their own marketing. Their pain: they know a lead magnet is the cheapest way to fill a pipeline, but they burn weeks building the wrong one (a 40-page ebook nobody finishes) or stall at the blank page. This tool forces the decision with a scoring rubric, then walks the winner to launch.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Stop guessing which lead magnet to build — score every idea on pain × speed × ease and ship the winner in a week."
2. "The lead-magnet workbench: concept board, conversion scoring, outline, landing copy, and launch checklist in one private file."
3. "A tiny lab for list growth: invent 10 magnet ideas with Claude, assay them like a scientist, launch the one that converts."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39.** It is a focused single-purpose tool that replaces a messy Notion doc plus a copywriting swipe; $29 is the sweet spot for impulse purchase by a consultant who bills $150/hr.
- Optional tiers: $19 app only · $39 app + "Magnet Recipes" pack (10 pre-scored concept templates by niche) · $79 bundle with 2–3 sibling BizDev-30 apps (e.g. LinkedIn Cadence Planner, ICP Architect).

## Launch checklist

1. Record a 60–90s demo GIF/video: load demo → score sliders moving the dial → live landing-page preview → export.
2. Take 4 screenshots (Bench with dials, Outline, Page Label preview, Copilot prompt open) on the demo data.
3. Write the landing page **using the app itself** — screenshot that meta-loop; it is the best proof.
4. Price at $29 with a launch coupon ($19 first week).
5. Post the build story + GIF on LinkedIn and X; the "score your magnet ideas on pain × speed × ease" rubric is the shareable hook.
6. Submit to niche directories and newsletters (indie tools, marketing-stack roundups, r/Entrepreneur-adjacent communities that allow tools).
7. Follow up with every buyer at day 3: ask what they launched — harvest testimonials for the page.

## Upsell paths

- **Done-with-you session ($150–$300):** 90 minutes on a call scoring their concept board and drafting the outline together.
- **Magnet Recipes packs ($9–$19):** pre-filled JSON imports of scored concepts + outlines per niche (agencies, coaches, SaaS).
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned variants ("Lead Magnet Lab for Accountants") with tailored demo data and checklist steps.
- Cross-sell the BizDev-30 suite as a bundle once 3+ apps are live.
