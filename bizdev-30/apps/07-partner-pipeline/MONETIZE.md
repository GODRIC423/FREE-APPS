# Partner Pipeline — How to sell it

## Who buys it

Bootstrapped SaaS founders, agency owners, and fractional/solo BD consultants who know channel partnerships could be their cheapest growth lever — but track them in a dead Notion page or not at all. The pain: promising partners go cold because there's no board, no fit discipline, and no next action.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "A harbor for your partnerships — score the fit, work the lanes, never let a warm partner go cold."
2. "The partnerships CRM that fits in one HTML file — no login, no monthly fee, yours forever."
3. "From 'we should partner sometime' to a signed rev-share — with Claude as your first mate."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39.** It replaces a spreadsheet plus an hour of consultant thinking per partner; single-file delivery justifies impulse-buy pricing, and the embedded Copilot prompts are the premium hook.
- Optional tiers: $19 app only · $39 app + "Partnership Playbook" PDF (angle library, rev-share benchmarks) · $99 bundle with sibling BD apps (Referral Engine, Battlecard Bay).

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest start, pay-what-you-want option for launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout + EU VAT handled.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives.
- List the demo harbor as screenshots everywhere; the visual identity is the ad.

## Launch checklist

1. Capture 4 screenshots: full board with demo data, the Cargo Manifest drawer with the fit dial, the First Mate panel with a generated prompt, the Markdown export in a text editor.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF/video: load demo → drag a card to Active → generate the rev-share prompt → copy.
3. Write the landing page around one line: "Partnerships die of silence. Give yours a harbor."
4. Post the build story on LinkedIn and indie-hacker communities (r/Entrepreneur, Indie Hackers, small-bets Discords) with the GIF.
5. Offer 20 launch copies at half price for testimonials; add the best two to the page.
6. Publish one free artifact from the app (e.g. "8-partner demo manifest" Markdown) as a lead magnet linking back.
7. Ship a v1.1 within two weeks based on buyer feedback and email every buyer — file delivery makes updates a selling point.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($150–$300):** you load their first 15 partners, score fit with them on a call, and hand back a worked board.
- **Template packs:** intro-email packs and rev-share term sheets per niche (agencies, newsletters, app marketplaces).
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned presets for Shopify app makers, podcasters, or MSPs (different kinds, demo data, prompt tuning).
- **Bundle:** cross-sell the other BizDev-30 apps to every buyer at a bundle discount.

## License note

Buyer receives the single self-contained `index.html` plus source (`src/`). Suggested terms: single-user license — use on any of your own devices and for your own business; no resale or redistribution; team licenses at 3× single price. Be honest in the listing: local-only tool, no accounts, no server, data stays in the buyer's browser.
