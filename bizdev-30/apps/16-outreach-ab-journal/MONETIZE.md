# Monetizing Outreach A/B Journal

## Who buys it

Founders doing their own outbound, freelance SDRs/agencies, and small sales teams (1–10 seats) who send hundreds of cold emails/DMs a month but decide "what's working" by vibes. Their pain: they keep rewriting messages without evidence, ship fake winners off 15-send samples, and lose the learnings when the person who ran the test leaves. Existing A/B tooling lives inside expensive sequencers they don't use.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Stop guessing which cold email works — a lab notebook that tells you when a winner is real."
2. "Your outreach experiments, with the statistics done honestly — no API, no subscription, yours forever."
3. "Turn every send into evidence: log A/B trials, get confidence-checked verdicts, build a proven playbook."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39.** It replaces a spreadsheet plus a statistics blog post, saves hours of setup, and the "honest small-sample math" is a genuine differentiator; under $40 is an impulse buy for anyone whose time is billable.
- Optional tiers: **$19** single-user file · **$49** "Team pack" (license for up to 5 people + your CSV conventions doc) · **$99** bundle with 2–3 sibling BD apps (e.g. Cold Email Forge, Cadence Composer).

## Where to sell (free to start)

- **Gumroad** — fastest start, discover feed, pay-what-you-want option for launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout + EU VAT handled if you sell to Europe.
- **Payhip / Ko-fi** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you already post outreach content.
- Direct via your own landing page later; start on a marketplace to validate.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo data and capture 3–4 crisp screenshots: a significant-winner card, the small-sample warning, the Insight board, a Copilot prompt preview.
2. Record a 30–60s GIF/video: log a trial → +1 replies → honesty strip flips to "B is ahead (~95%)" → pin the learning.
3. Write the landing copy around one promise: "know when a winner is real" (steal from Positioning above); include the demo screenshots and the shortcut list.
4. Price at $19 launch / $29 regular; enable PWYW above $19 for the first 100 buyers.
5. Post a teardown thread ("we reversed two 'winning' subject lines once the sample grew — here's the math") on LinkedIn, r/sales, r/Entrepreneur, and Indie Hackers, with the product linked at the end.
6. Ship the file + GUIDE.md as the download; add a "reply to this email with your journal JSON for a free critique" bonus to start conversations.
7. Collect 3 testimonials from early buyers (offer a free niche edition in exchange) and add them to the page.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup + coaching call ($150–$300):** design their first 3 experiments and sample-size plan with them.
- **Template packs ($9–$19):** pre-loaded journal JSONs — "SaaS founder pack", "Agency pack" — with proven hypotheses to test.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned copy and demo data for recruiters, fundraisers, podcast bookers.
- **Quarterly "playbook compile" service:** they send their JSON, you return a polished playbook doc.
