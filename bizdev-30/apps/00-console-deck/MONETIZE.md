# Console Deck — Selling the Bundle

Console Deck is not a 31st tool — it is the reason to buy all 30 at once. Individually the apps are $19–$49 utilities; racked into one console with shared connectors, they become a *suite*: set your profile, roster, and voice once, and every tool inherits it. Sell the console only as the spine of the bundle, never alone (it is an empty chassis without cartridges).

## Who buys it

Solo consultants, fractional executives, and 1–5 person agencies who live in business development all day. Their pain: their "BD stack" is fifteen scattered docs and spreadsheets that never share context, and every SaaS alternative is another $30–$90/month subscription. A one-time, local-first suite that runs on the $20 Claude subscription they already pay for is a category of one.

## Positioning (bundle-first)

- "Thirty BD tools. One console. Zero subscriptions — your whole pipeline stack, owned outright."
- "The BizDev-30 Deck: set your ICP, roster, and voice once; every tool in the rack already knows your business."
- "A complete business-development operating console that pairs with the $20 Claude subscription you already have."

## Pricing tiers

| Tier | Contents | Suggested price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Single cartridge** | Any one app + its GUIDE/MONETIZE docs | $19–$49 one-time |
| **Starter Rack** | Console Deck + any 5 cartridges (buyer picks) | $79–$99 one-time |
| **Full Deck** (flagship) | Console Deck + all 30 cartridges + all guides | $149–$249 one-time |
| **Full Deck + Setup** | Full Deck + 60-min setup call (patch their real profile, roster, ICP) | $349–$499 |

Rationale: 30 apps at even $19 each anchors a $570 "value", making $199 feel like a steal; one-time pricing is the wedge against $50/month SaaS (breaks even in ~4 months). Keep the console exclusive to bundles — it is the upgrade trigger that converts single-cartridge buyers ("your cartridge works better in the Deck").

## Bundle positioning mechanics

- Lead every listing with a 30-second video: insert cartridge → LEDs green → swap apps → context follows. The "connections persist across swaps" moment IS the demo.
- Sell single cartridges deliberately as tripwires; include a "Racked in the Console Deck" note + upgrade coupon (price paid credited toward Full Deck) inside each.
- Name the SKUs like hardware: "Full Deck", "Starter Rack", "Cartridge". The hardware metaphor makes one-time ownership feel natural.

## Where to sell free-to-start

Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, or Ko-fi (all no monthly fee; they take a cut per sale). Deliver as a zip of the `bizdev-30` folder — the console needs its sibling app folders, so always ship the full structure, even in Starter Rack (lock unpurchased cartridges by omitting their folders; the console's "cartridge not seated" screen handles absences gracefully and doubles as an upsell surface).

## Launch checklist

1. Record the 30-second insert/swap/LED demo GIF and a 2-minute full walkthrough video.
2. Screenshot set: patch board all-green, INSERT CARTRIDGE screen, three different cartridges seated, roster drawer.
3. Landing copy: lead with "own your BD stack outright", anchor against $570 individual value and SaaS subscriptions.
4. Load the demo patch before every screenshot/video so LEDs are green and data looks real.
5. Ship the zip with a top-level START-HERE.html pointing at the console; test the zip cold on a machine with no dev tools.
6. Launch posts where solo consultants gather: LinkedIn, r/consulting, r/Entrepreneur, Indie Hackers; offer a launch-week price.
7. Follow up single-cartridge buyers at day 7 with the Full Deck upgrade coupon.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service** ($150–$300): patch their real profile/roster/ICP live and configure three cartridges for their workflow.
- **Niche editions**: pre-loaded demo patches and copy for verticals (agencies, fractional CFOs, dev shops) at +$50.
- **Roster template packs**: industry account-list CSVs formatted for the roster importer.
- **Team license**: 5-seat internal license at 3x single price.
- **Update pass**: optional yearly "new cartridges + refinements" repurchase at ~40% off.

## License note

Be honest in listings: local-first tools with no accounts mean no lock-in and no recurring revenue promises — the pitch is ownership, not outcomes.
