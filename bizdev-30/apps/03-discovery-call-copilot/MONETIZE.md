# Discovery Call Copilot — How to Sell It

## Who buys it

Founders doing founder-led sales, solo consultants, fractional execs, and first sales hires at bootstrapped B2B companies. Their pain: discovery calls are improvised, notes end up scattered across notepads and half-filled CRM fields, they routinely hang up without a next step or a quantified pain, and they can't tell which calls were actually good. They won't pay for (or configure) Gong/Chorus-class call intelligence — but they'll pay once for a tool that makes every call scripted, timed, and scored.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Run discovery like a broadcast — scripted, timed, scored. Never hang up without the answers that close deals."
2. "The pre-call sheet, live teleprompter, and post-call scorecard for people who sell without a sales team."
3. "Gong tells you what happened. Discovery Call Copilot makes sure it happens — for a one-time price and zero recordings."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $29–$49.** It's a single-file tool with no hosting costs and clear, repeatable value per call; $29 is an impulse buy for anyone whose deals are worth four figures, $49 is defensible with the Claude prompt pack framed as included coaching.
- **Optional tiers:** $29 solo license · $79 team pack (5 seats + your company's starter question rack pre-loaded) · $149 "BD suite" bundle with 4–6 sibling apps from this collection.
- Launch at a $19 early-bird for the first 50 sales to harvest testimonials.

## Where to sell (free to start)

- **Gumroad** — fastest to launch; supports pay-what-you-want early-bird and license keys.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, EU VAT handled, good for the team-pack tier.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you already have an audience there.
- Distribution beyond the storefront: list it in indie-tool directories and "sales tools" roundups once you have screenshots.

## Launch checklist

1. Screenshot set: hero header with the console faceplate, live mode with the ON AIR lamp lit, the Signal Score dial on the demo debrief, the Producer's Booth. Dark felt + brass photographs beautifully — use it.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF/video: load demo → go on air → type a note → off air → dial swings → copy follow-up prompt into claude.ai.
3. Landing copy: lead with the pain ("You hung up without a next step. Again."), then the 4-band method, then the scorecard, then the Claude pairing ("works with the $20 Claude plan — no API keys").
4. Ship the demo scenario as your interactive demo: a Loom walking through the Meridian Freight debrief converts better than feature lists.
5. Post where the buyers are: r/sales, r/consulting, Indie Hackers, LinkedIn (use the app to prep a real call, post the scorecard), and founder-led-sales newsletters.
6. Collect 3–5 testimonials from early-bird buyers; put one next to the buy button.
7. Add a "powered by your own claude.ai subscription" FAQ entry — it pre-answers the "is there AI pricing?" objection.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($99–$199):** a 45-minute session where you build the buyer's question rack and first run sheet with them.
- **Question rack packs ($9–$19):** pre-built racks per niche — SaaS, agencies, IT services, industrial, healthcare — importable as JSON.
- **Niche editions:** re-skin + tailored bands and checklist (e.g., "Discovery Call Copilot for Recruiters" with intake calls) at the same price to new audiences.
- **Team onboarding ($299):** load the team's shared rack, train the momentum checklist as a qualification standard, set up a weekly debrief-review ritual.
