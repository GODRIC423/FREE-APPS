# Case Study Factory — Monetization Playbook

## Who buys it

Solo consultants, boutique agencies, and freelancers (design, dev, marketing, ops) who win real results for clients but have zero written proof — their "portfolio" is a folder of Slack screenshots. They know case studies close deals; they never write them because the blank page, the structure, and the awkward consent question all get in the way. This tool removes all three.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Turn last quarter's client win into a publishable case study this afternoon — interview, story, and consent, in one tool."
2. "The case-study pipeline for people who hate writing case studies: capture raw, assemble beats, ship three lengths."
3. "Proof beats promises. Factory-line your client wins into loglines, trailers, and full features — with the legal box ticked."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $29–$49.** It replaces a 2–4 hour writing task that consultants bill $100+/hr for; a single produced case study pays for it many times over. Under $50 keeps it an impulse buy for solo buyers.
- Optional tiers: **$29** single-user · **$79** studio license (whole team) · **$129** bundle with 2–3 sibling BD apps (e.g., Testimonial Harvester, Proposal Studio) as a "Proof Pack".

## Where to sell (free to start)

- **Gumroad** — best discovery for indie productivity tools; supports pay-what-you-want launches.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, handles EU VAT.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives.
- Your own site with a Gumroad/LS embed once the listing converts.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo, take 4 crisp screenshots (hero marquee, Edit Bay, Screening Room, Clearances) at 1440px on the dark set — the film-studio look is the thumbnail hook.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF/video: Load demo → beats fill → copy the Feature cut → the Writers' Room prompt into Claude.
3. Write the landing page with the tool itself: the page *is* a case study of the demo study (meta, and it proves the output quality).
4. Price at $29 launch / $39 regular; publish on Gumroad + Lemon Squeezy the same day.
5. Post the "1.1% → 5.4%" demo story on LinkedIn with the GIF; end with "the tool that wrote this is $29".
6. Submit to indie tool directories and relevant subreddits/communities for freelancers and agencies (r/freelance, Indie Hackers, agency Slack groups).
7. Email past clients/network: offer a free copy in exchange for a testimonial about the *output* (a finished case study).

## Upsell paths

- **Done-with-you setup ($150–$300):** run the client interview live with the buyer and produce their first case study together.
- **Beat-pack templates ($9–$19):** pre-written beat skeletons per niche (SaaS dev shop, marketing agency, fractional CFO).
- **Niche editions:** rebrand + tailored demo data for verticals (legal, healthcare consulting) at a higher price.
- **Proof Pack bundle:** cross-sell with Testimonial Harvester and Proposal Studio.
