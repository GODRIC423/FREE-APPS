# Niche Validator — How to sell it

## Who buys it

Solo consultants, freelancers, and small agency founders standing at a fork before they build a go-to-market motion: which niche do I actually go after? Their pain is real but underserved — niche selection frameworks live scattered across blog posts and Twitter threads, "market research" tools are built for enterprise analysts with five-figure budgets, and most people just pick the niche that feels exciting and find out six months later it was a mistake. They want a structured way to weigh candidates and force themselves to log evidence instead of vibes, without hiring a consultant or opening a 40-tab spreadsheet.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Pick the niche before you build the pipeline — weigh it, don't just feel it."
2. "Your niche isn't validated because it feels exciting. It's validated because you have the evidence stamped in writing."
3. "The gold-assay office for your next niche: score it, log the evidence, stamp the decision."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39** for a single-user license. Rationale: this is a decision-support tool used intensely for a week or two around a single pivotal choice, not a daily-driver SaaS — price it as an impulse buy that pays for itself the moment it prevents one wasted quarter chasing the wrong niche.
- Optional tiers: **$19 Solo** (the app) / **$49 Consultant Pack** (adds rebrand-and-deploy rights for client discovery engagements, a natural fit for fractional strategists and coaches who run niche-selection workshops) / bundle with a sibling BizDev tool (e.g. Lead Magnet Lab or ICP Architect) at a discount for buyers moving from "pick the niche" to "build the pipeline."

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest to launch, good discoverability among indie-hacker and solopreneur audiences who are exactly this buyer.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, handles EU VAT if selling to European consultants and coaches.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you build an audience around niche-selection or "how I picked my niche" content.
- **AppSumo Marketplace** later, once you have a handful of reviews — its audience over-indexes on exactly this "which niche should I chase" anxiety.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo assay and capture 4 screenshots: the balance-scale hero with two niches head to head, a specimen detail view with the karat gauge and evidence log, the side-by-side comparison table, and the stamped decision record.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF/video: dragging a score slider and watching the karat gauge move live, then the VALIDATED stamp animating onto a specimen.
3. Write the landing page around one story: "I almost built a business around the wrong niche — here's the board that stopped me" — then the three pitches above as sections.
4. Publish on Gumroad with launch pricing ($9–$12 first week), the license note below, and the stamp GIF above the fold.
5. Post the build story on r/entrepreneur, r/consulting, and Indie Hackers: "I built a tool that makes me argue against my own favorite niche before I commit to it" — link in profile/comments, not as a raw pitch.
6. Ship a free companion: "5 questions that kill a bad niche in one conversation" as a short PDF/checklist lead magnet that links to the paid app.
7. Answer "how do I pick a niche" questions in freelancer and agency-owner communities (Slack groups, Discord, Twitter/X) with genuinely useful frameworks pulled from the app's five criteria; link the tool only when it's actually the right answer.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($75–$200):** a 45-minute working session where you help a buyer score their first 3–5 real candidate niches live, including running the Copilot steelman-against prompt together.
- **Niche packs ($9–$19):** pre-built candidate lists with starter evidence prompts for specific buyer types — "consultants," "coaches," "agencies," "freelance developers" — importable JSON that jump-starts the board.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned builds for adjacent audiences — course creators picking a topic, newsletter writers picking a beat, VCs doing early-stage thesis screens — each with its own demo data and renamed criteria.
- **Workshop kit:** bundle the app with a short slide deck and facilitator script so career coaches and business consultants can run a "pick your niche" live session using the tool as the shared artifact.
