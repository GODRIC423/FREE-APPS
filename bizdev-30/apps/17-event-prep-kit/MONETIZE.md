# Monetizing Event Prep Kit

## Who buys it

Founders, BD reps, consultants, and agency owners who pay real money (booth fees, travel, sponsorships) to attend conferences, trade shows, and meetups but walk away with a stack of business cards and no system. Their pain: the pipeline value of an event is decided in the 48 hours after it ends, and that window is almost always lost to jet lag, inbox backlog, and scribbled notes nobody can read. This gives them a dossier before, a fast capture system during, and a queue that actually gets worked after — plus honest math on whether the next invite is worth the ticket.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Most people attend a conference. You're going to extract pipeline from it — badge to booked meeting, no scribbles lost."
2. "The 48-hour follow-up window decides your event ROI. This is the kit built entirely around not missing it."
3. "Stop guessing if events pay off — log the cost, log the conversations, and let the pipeline-multiple dial tell you the truth."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$35.** It's a single-file tool with no hosting cost and no subscription; $25 sits comfortably as an impulse buy for anyone about to spend $2,000+ on a conference trip.
- Optional tiers: **$19** app only · **$35** app + a printable "Conference Playbook" one-pager (pre-event checklist, day-of rules, 48-hour follow-up rules) · **$69** five-seat team license for BD teams attending together.
- Bundle candidate: pair with a cold-outreach or follow-up-cadence app from the same suite (e.g. "Extract, then close" bundle) at $49.

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest start, discover feed, good for a pay-what-you-want launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, handles EU VAT if that's part of your audience.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you already have an audience there.
- Your own site later once there's traction; start where checkout already exists.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo event and take 4–5 crisp screenshots: the target-badge wall, Day-of big-type mode, the follow-up ticket-stub queue, the ROI funnel + pipeline dial, and the Claude Copilot panel.
2. Record a 30–45 second GIF: stamp a target MET, scribble a note, queue the follow-up, watch the ROI dial move.
3. Write landing copy around the pain ("your event ROI is decided in 48 hours, and you're losing that window"), not the feature list; A/B test the three positioning lines as headlines.
4. Publish on Gumroad with the demo GIF as the cover image; price at $25 with a $19 launch-week discount.
5. Post a build-in-public thread timed to an actual upcoming conference season (spring/fall B2B event calendars) showing a real dossier being built.
6. Submit to niche communities and directories: r/sales, r/Entrepreneur (rules permitting), Indie Hackers, conference/event-marketing newsletters, and sales-tool roundup sites.
7. Offer the first 10 buyers a free review of their target list and talk tracks in exchange for a testimonial after their next event.

## Upsell paths

- **Done-with-you setup:** a paid 30-minute session prepping the buyer's dossier for a specific upcoming event, live ($75–$200).
- **Template packs:** pre-written talk-track and icebreaker libraries by vertical (SaaS, agencies, professional services, healthcare) at $9–$19 each.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned variants for specific audiences ("Trade Show Prep Kit" for exhibitors, "Meetup Prep Kit" for community-led growth) with tailored demo data.
- **The suite:** graduate buyers into the full 30-app BizDev bundle, positioning this as the "field" companion to the outreach and cadence tools.
