# Conference ROI Planner — How to sell it

## Who buys it

Founders, heads of marketing/BD, and sales leaders at small-to-mid B2B companies (2-50 people) who attend or exhibit at 3-15 events a year and currently decide with a gut check and a shared spreadsheet nobody updates after the fact. Their pain: a $15-40k trade-show booth commitment gets approved on FOMO ("our competitor will be there"), nobody models the pipeline math before signing the check, and nobody closes the loop after the event to see if it actually worked — so the same mistake repeats next year. They want a five-minute forcing function that turns "should we go?" into a number, and a paper trail for when finance asks why.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Decide events with math, not FOMO — the breakeven model for every conference on your list."
2. "Stop guessing your booth ROI. Model the pipeline, see the breakeven line, make the call before you buy the ticket."
3. "Every event is a bet. Log the cost, forecast the pipeline, and let the runway bar tell you if it pays for itself."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19-$39** for a single-user license. Rationale: it replaces a spreadsheet and a recurring budget-approval headache, not a full event-management platform (Bizzabo, Cvent-style tools are enterprise, five-figure annual, and solve logistics, not the go/no-go decision). Priced as a low-friction impulse buy for a founder or marketing lead heading into conference season.
- Optional tiers: **$29 Solo** (the app) / **$59 Team Pack** (license for up to 5 people, shared demo flight log as a training example) / **$99 Agency/Consultant** (adds the right to run the flight-log exercise live with client teams, not resell the app itself).

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Lemon Squeezy** — clean checkout and EU VAT handling if selling to European marketing/BD teams.

- **AppSumo Marketplace** as a later listing once you have reviews and a case study or two.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo flight log and capture 4 screenshots: the Flight Log with the six-event route hero, a dossier's Breakeven & Decision tab (ROI altitude tape + runway bar), the Post-Event Actuals plan-vs-actual table, and the Claude Copilot panel with a generated prompt.
2. Record a 30-45s GIF: type in a booth cost and ticket price → move the pipeline sliders and watch the ROI altitude tape swing from red to green → click GO and watch the decision record save.
3. Write the landing page around one story: "the $22k booth we almost bought on vibes" — walk through the breakeven math that changed the decision, then the three pitches above as sections, with the runway bar (cost vs. expected revenue) as the hero visual.
4. Publish on Gumroad with launch pricing ($12-$15 first week), the license note below, and the GIF above the fold.
5. Post the build story in marketing-ops and founder communities (r/marketing, r/Entrepreneur, Marketing Ops Slack groups, IndieHackers) with the honest angle: "we stopped approving events on FOMO — here's the tool" plus the ROI gauge screenshot.
6. Ship a free companion lead magnet: a one-page "5 numbers to know before you say yes to a booth" checklist (a condensed version of the cost-model fields) that links to the paid app.
7. Answer trade-show-budget and event-ROI questions in marketing/BD subreddits and LinkedIn threads with genuinely useful math, not a pitch; link the tool in your profile.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($75-$200):** a 30-45 minute call where you build out the buyer's real upcoming event slate — cost models, pipeline assumptions calibrated to their actual close rates — and record the first round of go/no-go decisions live.
- **Vertical demo packs ($15-$25):** pre-loaded flight logs for specific event circuits (SaaS conference season, healthcare trade shows, professional-services summits) with realistic cost/pipeline numbers as a starting template.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned builds for agencies or fractional CMOs who run this exercise across multiple client accounts, each with a client-specific demo log.
- **Quarterly event-budget retainer:** for fractional marketing/BD leaders — run the *pressure-test* and *post-event report* Copilot prompts across a client's event calendar every quarter and deliver the readout as a paid service.
