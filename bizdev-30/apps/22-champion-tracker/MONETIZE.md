# Champion Tracker — How to sell it

## Who buys it

Account executives and founder-led sellers carrying complex B2B deals — anywhere a purchase needs more than one internal "yes." Their pain: they can name the person who likes them, but not the person who can actually sign, and they find out too late that their one warm contact just left the company. Enterprise stakeholder-mapping features live buried inside Gong, Salesforce Sales Cloud, or MEDDICC coaching platforms that cost hundreds a month and demand a rollout; this buyer wants a working champion map for one account, today, in a single file.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Map your stakeholders before your best contact goes quiet — a champion map that doesn't need a CRM rollout."
2. "Every deal you lost to silence had a warning sign. Champion Tracker puts it on the screen before the deal dies."
3. "Your account plan + a $20 Claude subscription = a deal-risk analyst who reads your stakeholder map for you."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $29–$49** for a single-user license. Rationale: it replaces an account-planning spreadsheet nobody keeps updated, not a SaaS seat; priced to be an easy yes for a rep who has felt a deal go single-threaded at least once.
- Optional tiers: **$29 Solo** (the app) / **$79 Team Pack** (license for up to 5 reps, useful for a sales-led agency or small SaaS team standardizing account plans) / **$149 Sales-Leader Edition** (adds a facilitator's guide for running the champion-readiness checklist as a weekly deal-review ritual).

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest to launch, built-in discoverability, pay-what-you-want option for launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout and EU VAT handling if selling to European sales consultants.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi suits a builder who publishes sales-process content.
- Bundle listing on **AppSumo Marketplace** later, once reviews exist.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo constellation and capture 4 screenshots: the full stakeholder map with connecting lines, an open stakeholder editor with sliders, the champion-readiness dial at a strong score, and a threading-risk list with multiple flags.
2. Record a 30–45s screen capture: drag a stakeholder from Bystander into Blocker territory, watch a risk flag appear, then click *Queue action* to fix it.
3. Write the landing page around one story: "the deal that died when my only contact left the company" — then the three pitches above as sections.
4. Publish on Gumroad with launch pricing ($19 first week), a plain-language license note, and the drag-to-risk-flag GIF above the fold.
5. Post the build story on LinkedIn and in sales communities (r/sales, RevGenius, Pavilion) where "single-threaded deal" is a phrase people already use — attach the constellation screenshot, it reads well at a glance.
6. Ship a free companion: an 8-question "Is your champion actually a champion?" checklist as a one-page PDF lead magnet that links to the paid app.
7. Answer stakeholder-mapping and MEDDICC questions in sales forums with genuinely useful advice; link the tool in your profile, not inside the answers.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($150–$300):** a working session where you map the buyer's current top 3 accounts live, using the Copilot threading-risk prompt as the diagnostic.
- **Vertical demo packs ($15–$29):** pre-populated example constellations for a specific motion — enterprise SaaS, agency retainers, healthcare IT (like the bundled demo) — importable JSON that shows "done well" for that world.
- **Sales-leader edition:** a facilitator's guide plus a print-ready weekly deal-review template built around the risk-flag categories, sold to team leads running pipeline reviews.
- **Quarterly account-plan retainer:** for fractional sales leaders — run the threading-risk and champion-enablement Copilot prompts across a client's top accounts every quarter.
