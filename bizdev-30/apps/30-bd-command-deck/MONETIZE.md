# BD Command Deck — How to sell it

## Who buys it

Solo founders, freelance consultants, agency owners, and small BD/sales teams (1–10 people) who are personally responsible for their own pipeline and currently track it in a spreadsheet, a notes app, or nowhere at all. Their pain: BD activity happens in bursts and stalls, they can't say with a straight face whether last week was good or bad, and they have no ritual that forces a Friday review. They want a weekly forcing function that turns raw activity numbers into an honest read on their funnel — not a CRM migration project.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Run business development like a bridge watch — five numbers, one streak, one weakest stage, every week."
2. "Stop guessing whether last week was good. The scorecard that tells you where your funnel actually leaks."
3. "Your weekly BD ritual + a $20 Claude subscription = a revenue-ops analyst reading your numbers every Friday."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39** for a single-user license. Rationale: it replaces a personal spreadsheet and a habit you keep meaning to build, not a CRM or forecasting platform; priced as an easy impulse buy for a solo operator, well under any team-wide sales analytics tool.
- Optional tiers: **$19 Solo** (the app) / **$49 Team Pack** (license for up to 5 seats, shared target-calibration presets) / **$89 Coach Edition** (adds a facilitator's guide for running the weekly review ritual with clients, plus the quarter-report print layout formatted for client hand-off).

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest to launch, strong existing audience of indie-tool and solopreneur buyers, easy pay-what-you-want for a launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout and EU VAT handling if you expect European freelancer/agency buyers.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi** — zero-monthly-fee alternatives; Ko-fi suits a creator who already posts about their own BD numbers publicly.
- **AppSumo Marketplace** as a later listing once you have reviews and a track record.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo run and capture 4 screenshots: the instrument cluster mid-week with all five gauges lit green, the conversion waterfall with a weakest stage called out in red, the signal-flag streak strip at 7 weeks, and the Quarter Report with quarter-over-quarter deltas.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF: type a number into a gauge's input field and watch the needle sweep and the waterfall recompute live, then cut to checking off a "next bet" and watching the ritual badge flip to "logged."
3. Write the landing page around one story: "the week I finally knew why my pipeline was dry" — lead with the weakest-stage diagnosis, then the three pitches above as sections, with the conversion waterfall as the hero visual.
4. Publish on Gumroad with launch pricing ($12–$15 first week), the license note below, and the GIF above the fold.
5. Post the build story in solopreneur and indie-hacker communities (IndieHackers, r/smallbusiness, r/freelance, founder Slack/Discord groups) with the waterfall screenshot: "I built myself a bridge console for my BD numbers" — genuinely useful framing, not a hard pitch.
6. Ship a free companion lead magnet: a one-page "5-number weekly BD scorecard" PDF (just the metric definitions and target-setting guidance, no software) that links to the paid app for anyone who wants it automated.
7. Answer "how do I know if my outreach is working" and "how many cold emails should I send a week" questions in relevant subreddits and founder communities with genuinely useful advice; link the tool in your profile, not inside the answer.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($75–$150):** a 30-minute call to calibrate targets to the buyer's actual sales motion and backfill their last 4–6 weeks of numbers so the streak and trend rail have real history on day one.
- **Coach/consultant packs ($29–$49):** a facilitator's guide for running the weekly review ritual with multiple clients, plus the Quarter Report print layout pre-formatted as a client-ready recap.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned builds for specific practices (agencies, recruiters, consultants) with domain-appropriate metric labels and a matching demo run.
- **Quarterly accountability retainer:** for fractional sales leaders or coaches — run the *Accountability update* and *Synthesize my review ritual* Copilot prompts with a client's actual data every quarter and deliver the readout as a paid service.
