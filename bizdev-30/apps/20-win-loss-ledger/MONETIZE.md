# Monetizing Win/Loss Ledger

## Who buys it

Solo founders, agency owners, and small B2B sales teams (roughly 1–15 sellers) who close enough deals to have real patterns but don't have — or don't want — a Salesforce/Gong-grade win/loss program. The pain is specific: leadership *knows* they're losing to the same competitor or the same objection repeatedly, but nobody writes it down, so every quarterly review is a rehash of gut feelings instead of evidence. A fractional sales consultant or RevOps freelancer running win/loss programs for multiple clients is also a strong buyer — this tool is the deliverable they hand the client.

## Positioning

1. "Every closed deal has a lesson in it — if you write it down before the memory fades."
2. "The win/loss ledger your quarterly business review has been missing, minus the $30k consulting engagement."
3. "Stop guessing why you're losing to the same competitor. Log it once, see the pattern forever."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time price: $29–$49** for the single-user HTML file. This sits comfortably in the "solves a real recurring pain, low cognitive overhead to buy" impulse-purchase band for indie/small-team B2B tools.
- **Team/agency tier: $99–$149** — same file, license extended to internal use across a small team (see License note), plus a short setup call or Loom walkthrough.
- **Bundle:** pair with a complementary BD tool from this same suite (e.g., a deal-qualification or pipeline tool) at a 2-for-1 price around $59–$79 — the win/loss ledger is the natural "back half" of a qualification tool, closing the loop from forecast to outcome.

No revenue promises here — price testing on Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy over 2–4 weeks will tell you more than any guess.

## Launch checklist

1. **Screenshot the pattern dashboard and a filled-in deal card** — these two visuals sell the product faster than any copy; the SVG bar charts and the WON/LOST rubber-stamp treatment read as "real software," not a template.
2. **Record a 60–90 second demo GIF/video**: load the demo data, scroll the pattern dashboard, open a deal card, copy a Copilot prompt, paste the export. Show the whole loop, not just the UI.
3. **Write landing copy around the pain, not the features** — lead with "you're losing the same way twice" before listing "SVG bar charts" and "CSV export."
4. **Post in BD/RevOps-adjacent communities**: relevant subreddits (r/sales, r/Entrepreneur), the RevGenius and Pavilion Slack/community spaces, and any indie-hacker Twitter/X or LinkedIn audience you have — win/loss process is a recognizable pain point that generates real engagement, not just impressions.
5. **List on a "tools for founders/consultants" directory** (e.g., a relevant Notion/AppSumo-adjacent or indie tools directory) if you have access to one.
6. **Offer a free-tier limited version** (e.g., cap at 5 logged deals, or ship the demo-data-only version) as a lead magnet that upsells to the full unlocked file.
7. **Follow up with buyers after 2 weeks** asking what patterns they found — testimonials from real win-rate discoveries are the best possible future ad copy.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup/import service** — offer to migrate a buyer's existing spreadsheet of closed deals into the ledger's JSON format for a flat fee ($75–$150); this also surfaces you as a person who can consult on their actual win/loss process.
- **Vertical/niche editions** — pre-seed the demo data and driver vocabulary for a specific vertical (agencies, SaaS, professional services, franchise sales) and sell as a themed variant.
- **Facilitated win/loss interview add-on** — since the Copilot already generates an interview script, offer to actually *run* 3–5 win/loss interviews for a client as a paid service, using this tool as the intake and analysis layer.
- **Quarterly readout service** — sell a recurring engagement where you (or the buyer's ops person) fill the ledger and generate the quarterly readout each quarter; the tool becomes the deliverable engine for a retainer.
- **Template pack** — bundle a set of pre-written driver taxonomies and price-factor definitions tuned for different sales motions (PLG, enterprise, agency) as a paid add-on.

## License note

The buyer receives the single self-contained `index.html` file (all HTML/CSS/JS/fonts inlined — no external dependencies) plus the editable `src/` source (`App.jsx`, `styles.css`, `index.html`, `main.jsx`). Suggested license: **single-user / single-business license** — the buyer may use, modify, and deploy the file for their own business or for one named client engagement, but may not resell, sublicense, or redistribute the file or its source as a competing product. State clearly at purchase whether a "team" purchase covers internal seats only, and price any resale/reseller/agency-white-label rights separately and explicitly.
