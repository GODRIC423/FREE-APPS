# Deal Qualifier — How to sell it

## Who buys it

Individual quota-carrying AE/SDR reps, sales managers, and founder-sellers at small B2B companies (1–15 sellers) who run a MEDDICC-adjacent process but currently track it in a spreadsheet or a CRM field nobody fills in honestly. Their pain: forecast calls where deals are staged on optimism, not evidence, and they get burned every quarter by a "Commit" deal that had no economic buyer and no real champion. They want a forcing function that makes qualification gaps visible before the forecast meeting, not a CRM migration.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Qualify hard, forecast honestly — the MEDDICC checkpoint that catches hope deals before your VP does."
2. "Stop staging deals on vibes. Six lanes, evidence on file, a score you can defend."
3. "Your qualification discipline + a $20 Claude subscription = a deal inspector on every call."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $25–$49** for a single-user license. Rationale: it replaces a spreadsheet and a painful forecast-call habit, not a CRM; priced as an impulse buy for an individual rep or manager, well under any Clari/Gong-style forecasting platform (which are five-figure annual, team-wide, and require admin buy-in).
- Optional tiers: **$25 Solo** (the app) / **$69 Team Pack** (license for up to 5 reps, shared calibration presets) / **$129 Sales Leader** (adds a facilitator's guide for running the weekly forecast scrub with the Copilot audit prompt).

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout and EU VAT handling if selling to European sales teams.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo pipeline and capture 4 screenshots: the queue sorted by hope gap, a dossier mid-inspection on a pillar lane, the verdict stamp (CLEARED vs HELD AT GATE side by side), and the Claude Copilot panel with a generated prompt.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF: a deal staged "Commit" with a red hope-gap flag → walk one lane from No papers to Verified with evidence → watch the score dial and verdict stamp update live.
3. Write the landing page around one story: "the deal I called Commit that died in week 3" — then the three pitches above as sections, with the truth-meter (stated vs. evidence-weighted pipeline) as the hero visual.
4. Publish on Gumroad with launch pricing ($15–$19 first week), the license note below, and the GIF above the fold.
5. Post the build story on LinkedIn and in sales-community Slack/Discord groups (RevGenius, Pavilion-adjacent spaces): "I stopped trusting my own forecast until I built this" with the queue screenshot; follow up with a tip on reading hope gaps.
6. Ship a free companion lead magnet: a one-page MEDDICC probe cheat-sheet (the six lanes' "inspector's probes," reformatted as a PDF) that links to the paid app.
7. Answer forecast-accuracy and qualification questions in r/sales, r/salesengineering, and founder-sales communities with genuinely useful advice; link the tool in your profile, not inside the answers.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($100–$300):** a 60-minute call where you calibrate lane weights to the buyer's actual sales motion and qualify their top 5 open deals live using the Copilot interrogation prompt.
- **Cadence/manager packs ($15–$29):** pre-built weekly-forecast-ritual guides (agenda, the exact Copilot prompts to run in order, how to read the hope gap in a team review).
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned builds for agencies, consultancies, or recruiters selling retained engagements, each with domain-specific demo pipelines and probe language.
- **Quarterly forecast-scrub retainer:** for fractional sales leaders — run the *Audit my forecast* Copilot ritual with a client's team every quarter and deliver the readout.
