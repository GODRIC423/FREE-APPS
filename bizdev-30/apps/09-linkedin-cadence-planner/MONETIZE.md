# Monetizing LinkedIn Cadence Planner

## Who buys it

Solo consultants, fractional executives, agency founders, and B2B SaaS founders doing founder-led sales who already know LinkedIn drives their pipeline but post erratically — a burst of five posts when motivated, then three weeks of silence. Their pain: no system for turning "I should post more" into a repeatable weekly output, no way to see if their content mix is stale, and no discipline around the cheap, high-leverage work of commenting and resharing. This gives them a newsroom-grade production line: a hook bank, a fold-aware editor, a calendar with a cadence meter, and a wire queue — plus Claude prompts that write in their actual voice.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Run your LinkedIn like a newsroom, not a mood — hooks banked, posts filed, a wire that never goes quiet."
2. "The 210-character fold is the whole game. This is the only planner built to make you win it every time."
3. "Your $20 Claude subscription just became a ghostwriter — this is the composing desk that feeds it your real voice and your real month."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$35.** A single-file tool with no hosting cost and no recurring bill for the buyer; $25 is a comfortable impulse-buy price for a habit-forming content system that would otherwise cost a $200+/month scheduling SaaS.
- Optional tiers: **$19** app only · **$35** app + a "30-Day Cadence Kickstart" checklist (a printable plan for the first month of consistent posting) · **$69** five-seat team/agency license for managing several client voices.
- Bundle candidate: pair with a case-study or testimonial app from the same suite at $49 ("Write it, prove it, post it").

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest start, built-in discover feed, pay-what-you-want option works well for a launch-week push.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, handles EU VAT if that's relevant to your audience.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi is strong if you already have an audience there.
- Your own landing page later, once checkout infrastructure already exists elsewhere.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo edition and take 4–5 crisp screenshots: the front-page calendar, the hook bank, the post editor with both fold/length gauges visible, and the Rewrite Desk panel.
2. Record a 30–45 second screen capture: bank a hook → drag it into a draft → watch the fold gauge fill → copy a Copilot critique prompt.
3. Write landing copy around the pain ("you know you should post more — this is why you don't"), not the feature list; test the three positioning lines above as headline variants.
4. Publish on Gumroad with the screen capture as the cover asset; price at $25 with a $19 launch-week discount.
5. Post a build-in-public thread on LinkedIn itself (the obvious channel) showing a real week planned end to end inside the tool.
6. Submit to relevant communities and roundups: r/sales, r/Entrepreneur (per rules), Indie Hackers, BetaList-style newsletters, and LinkedIn-creator-tool listicles.
7. Offer the first 10 buyers a free 15-minute "cadence audit" call in exchange for a testimonial or a public review.

## Upsell paths

- **Done-with-you setup:** a paid session where you fill the buyer's editorial slate and bank their first 20 hooks live with them ($100–$250).
- **Template packs:** pre-filled hook banks and demo months per niche (agencies, coaches, recruiters, fractional execs) sold at $9–$19 each.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned variants ("Cadence Desk for Recruiters") with tailored demo data and hook-type language.
- **The suite:** graduate buyers into the full 30-app BizDev bundle for the rest of the pipeline, from ICP to close.
