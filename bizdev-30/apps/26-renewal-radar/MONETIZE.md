# Renewal Radar — How to sell it

## Who buys it

Solo customer success managers, fractional CS/CX consultants, and founder-led B2B companies (5–50 accounts) who own renewals themselves but have outgrown a spreadsheet. Their pain: renewals go quiet, "at risk" is a gut feeling nobody wrote down until the cancellation email arrives, and enterprise CS platforms (Gainsight, ChurnZero, Vitally) start at five figures a year with a mandatory sales call and a rollout project. They want a structured renewal cadence and an at-risk alarm this afternoon, not a platform evaluation.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Never lose a client to silence — the renewal radar Gainsight charges $20k/yr for, in one file you own."
2. "T-90, T-60, T-30 — the exact plays to run, before every renewal, with a save queue for the ones going quiet."
3. "Your renewal book + a $20 Claude subscription = a customer success analyst on demand."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $29–$59** for a single-user license. Rationale: it replaces a spreadsheet + a sticky note, not a SaaS platform; impulse-buy range for a tool that plausibly saves one renewal. Anchor against Gainsight/ChurnZero/Vitally annual pricing in the sales copy.
- Optional tiers: **$29 Solo** (the app) / **$79 Team Pack** (license for up to 5 CSMs, plus the four Copilot prompts as a standalone one-pager) / **$149 Consultant** (rebrand-and-deploy rights for fractional CS consultants running this inside client engagements).

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest to launch, built-in discoverability, pay-what-you-want option for launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout and EU VAT handled, useful if selling to European CS consultants.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you're building an audience around CS/renewals content.
- Bundle listing on **AppSumo Marketplace** later, once a few reviews exist.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo portfolio and capture 4 screenshots: the radar scope with mixed blips, the client detail view (health dial + save play), the At-risk queue tab, and the Copilot panel with a generated save-plan prompt.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF: an account drifting past 45 days of silence → the blip's dashed silence ring appears → status flips to At risk → save play opens.
3. Write the landing page around one story: "the renewal that went quiet and you found out from the cancellation email" — then the three pitches above as sections.
4. Publish on Gumroad with launch pricing ($19 first week), the license note below, and the GIF above the fold.
5. Post the build story on LinkedIn and in CS communities (r/customersuccess, CS Slack groups): "I built myself a renewal radar because I kept losing accounts to silence" with the scope screenshot.
6. Ship a free companion: "The T-90/T-60/T-30 renewal playbook" as a one-page PDF lead magnet that links to the paid app.
7. Answer renewal and churn-prevention questions in CS communities with genuinely useful specifics (e.g., the silence-threshold idea); link the tool in your profile, not the posts.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($150–$400):** a 90-minute call where you import the buyer's real account list, set health signals together, and flag their true at-risk accounts using the save-play Copilot prompt live.
- **Playbook packs ($15–$29):** pre-written T-90/T-60/T-30 play templates and save-play templates per vertical (SaaS, agency retainers, usage-based) as importable JSON.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned builds for agencies (retainer renewals), SaaS resellers, and membership businesses, each with domain-specific demo data.
- **Quarterly renewal-audit retainer:** for consultants — run the portfolio export + Copilot quarterly-readout style review with a client's CS team every quarter.
