# Expansion Matrix — Monetization Notes

## Who buys it

Small agency and consultancy owners (5–30 people) who already have a base of paying clients but no systematic way to see where the next dollar of revenue is hiding — they're relying on memory and gut feel about which client might want which service. Also fractional CROs/consultants and B2B SaaS account managers running land-and-expand motions who need a lightweight artifact (not a $150/seat/mo CRM module) to bring into a QBR or pipeline review. The pain is specific: expansion revenue is usually cheaper to win than new logos, but almost nobody maps it deliberately — it just happens (or doesn't) in someone's head.

## Positioning

1. "The whitespace grid your account reviews have been missing — every client, every offer, one honest cell each."
2. "Stop guessing which client wants what next. Score it, size it, and work your best cells first."
3. "A pipeline review artifact for teams too small for a real CRM and too serious to wing it."

## Suggested pricing

**$19–$29 one-time** for the single-user license. This sits comfortably in impulse-purchase territory for an agency owner browsing a marketplace, and matches the going rate for well-crafted single-purpose HTML tools (above a $5–9 "tip jar" tool, below a $49+ multi-user product). Because there's no server cost and no ongoing support obligation, margin is effectively 100% after the marketplace's cut.

Optional tiering:
- **Solo — $19**: the tool as-is, single-user license.
- **Team pack — $49**: same file, licensed for up to 5 people at one company (still just an HTML file each person opens locally — no real technical difference, priced for the internal-distribution convenience and the "we're buying it for the team" budget line).
- **Agency bundle** (see Upsell paths below) for buyers who want this alongside sibling BizDev tools.

## Where to sell (free-to-start, no monthly fee)

- **Gumroad** — best default for a single polished HTML deliverable; low friction checkout, built-in affiliate option.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — similar to Gumroad, handles VAT/tax automatically, good if selling to EU buyers.
- **Payhip** — simple, no monthly fee, decent for bundling multiple file formats (HTML + a PDF one-pager).
- **Ko-fi Shop** — good secondary channel if you already have an audience there (e.g., a newsletter or Twitter/X following of agency owners).

## Launch checklist

1. **Screenshots** — capture the loaded-demo state of: the full matrix (hero shot), the Cell Inspector open on a high-value cell, the Play Queue, and the Claude Copilot panel. These four cover "what it looks like" and "what it does" in one scroll.
2. **Demo GIF (10–15s)** — record clicking a cell, setting it to In Play, watching it appear in the Play Queue, then copying a Copilot prompt. This is the whole value prop in one loop.
3. **Landing copy** — lead with the pain ("you're leaving money in your own client base and you know it"), not the feature list. One sentence on what it is, three bullets on what it does, one line on privacy (local-only, no account, no subscription).
4. **Pricing page / product page** — state clearly: single HTML file, works offline, no login, no data leaves your device. Buyers evaluating small tools care about this more than almost anything else in the description.
5. **Post where agency owners already are** — r/agency, r/consulting, Indie Hackers "Show IH", relevant Slack/Discord communities for freelancers and small agencies, and a short thread on X/LinkedIn showing the demo GIF.
6. **Bundle-list it** — if you build more than one of the BizDev-30 tools, list Expansion Matrix inside a themed bundle ("Account Growth Kit": Expansion Matrix + Renewal Radar + Champion Tracker) at a bundle discount — bundles convert better than any single $19 item.
7. **Collect one testimonial before the second post.** DM 3–5 people from your own network to try it free in exchange for a two-sentence quote; use it on the product page before pushing the second wave of distribution.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service** — offer a $99–199 "we'll fill in your first matrix with you" 30-minute call for buyers who want a done-for-you first pass rather than a blank tool.
- **Template packs** — pre-filled demo scenarios for specific verticals (agency, dev shop, fractional-exec practice, B2B SaaS CS team) sold as a small add-on or included in a higher tier — buyers copy the JSON import and swap in their own names.
- **Niche editions** — a re-skinned build (different demo data, tuned copy) sold directly into a vertical community, e.g. "Expansion Matrix for Web Design Agencies" posted specifically where that audience gathers, same underlying tool.
- **Bundle with adjacent BizDev tools** — Territory Mapper (segment planning) and Renewal Radar (retention) are natural companions; a 3-tool "Grow the Base" bundle at a 30–40% discount to the sum of individual prices increases average order value.

## License note

Buyers receive the single self-contained `index.html` file (and, if you choose to include it, the editable `src/` source). Suggested license: **single-user / single-company license** — the buyer may use the tool for their own business or client work indefinitely, on any number of their own devices, but may not resell, redistribute, or relicense the file itself. A team-pack purchase extends usage rights to a named group at one company; it does not grant redistribution or resale rights. No warranty, no ongoing support obligation implied — clearly state that in the product listing to set expectations.
