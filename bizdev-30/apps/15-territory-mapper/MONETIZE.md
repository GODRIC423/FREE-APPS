# Territory Mapper — Monetization Playbook

## Who buys it

Founders doing founder-led sales, fractional VPs of Sales, and agency/consultancy owners with 1–5 people who can carry accounts. Their pain: they chase everything, tier nothing, and can't answer "where do we focus this quarter?" without a spreadsheet nobody maintains. CRMs assume the territory decision was already made — this tool *is* that decision.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "The map before the CRM — decide where to sell before you buy tools to sell with."
2. "Segment sizing, account tiering, and coverage math on one page — no login, no SaaS fee, works with the Claude subscription you already pay for."
3. "Stop spraying. Chart your territories, tier your accounts, and plant one flag per quarter."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $29–$49.** It replaces a messy spreadsheet plus an afternoon of consulting-grade thinking; buyers at this price don't need a demo call. $39 is the sweet spot for a single polished HTML tool with a Claude-copilot workflow baked in.
- Optional bundle: **BD Toolkit tier at $99–$149** when sold alongside sibling apps (ICP Architect, Deal Qualifier, Pricing Bench) — "the pre-CRM planning bench."
- Launch discount: first 100 at $19 to harvest testimonials.

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest, discovery traffic, pay-what-you-want option for launch.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout + EU VAT handled (merchant of record).
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi suits audience-first sellers.
- Deliver as a zip: `index.html` (self-contained) + `GUIDE.md` + source.

## Launch checklist

1. Screenshot set: the full chart with demo data, the coverage dials at 110% (the "you're pretending" moment), and a Copilot prompt in claude.ai with its answer.
2. Record a 45-second GIF/Loom: Load demo → click the flagged island → tier an account → copy the "Tune my tiering rules" prompt into Claude.
3. Landing copy: lead with the question ("Where should you actually focus next quarter?"), show the map, list the three Copilot actions, state "no login, no subscription, data never leaves your browser."
4. Post the demo chart (as an image) on LinkedIn with the coverage-math lesson: T1 is a budget, not an honor. Link in comments.
5. Submit to r/sales, r/agency, Indie Hackers, and a "tools for fractional sales leaders" newsletter; offer 5 free copies for feedback.
6. Ask the first 10 buyers one question — "what did the verdict tell you?" — and turn answers into testimonial cards.
7. Add a Gumroad upsell at checkout: the bundle, or a 30-minute territory-review call.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup service ($150–$300):** a 60-minute working session — chart their territories live, hand back the JSON.
- **Vertical editions:** pre-loaded demo charts + tuned Copilot prompts for agencies, MSPs, dev shops, recruiters.
- **Template pack:** 10 pre-sized segment libraries (US SMB SaaS, EU manufacturing, etc.) as importable JSONs.
- **Quarterly re-survey ritual:** a paid email course wrapping the app in a 4-week operating cadence.
