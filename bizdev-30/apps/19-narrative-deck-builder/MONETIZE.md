# Narrative Deck Builder — Monetization Playbook

## Who buys it

Founders, account executives, sales engineers, and consultants who pitch regularly and are tired of opening PowerPoint/Keynote/Google Slides before they've figured out what the story actually is. They'll pay for a tool that forces the arc — status quo, threat, promised land, proof, ask — into shape *before* they touch a slide, because a weak deck built fast is still a weak deck. Sales enablement teams and fractional CMOs who template pitches for a team are a strong secondary buyer.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Structure the story before you touch a single slide."
2. "The corkboard for your pitch — pin the arc, then let any deck tool catch up."
3. "Every good pitch has five beats. This is where you find out if yours are missing one."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $29–$45.** It's a focused, single-purpose thinking tool (not a full deck builder), so it sits below general-purpose presentation software but above a template pack — $29 is an easy yes for anyone billing $100+/hr who pitches monthly, $45 is defensible once framed as "cheaper than one wasted pitch."
- Optional tiers: **$29 Solo** (the app) / **$59 Team** (5 licenses + a shared demo board tuned to their category) / **$149 Agency** (unlimited internal seats + a 30-minute working session building their first real arc).
- Bundle: pair with sibling BD apps that touch the same pitch (Proposal Studio, Case Study Factory, Battlecard Bay) as a "Win the Deal" 3–4 pack at ~35–40% off combined price — the narrative arc naturally feeds proof and objection-handling in those tools.

- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, EU VAT handled (merchant of record).
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; good if you already have an audience on either.
- Your own landing page later; start where checkout is free and low-friction.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo data and take 4 screenshots: the hero + stat chips, an expanded beat card mid-edit, the Pacing check meter showing a clear verdict, and the Read-through view.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF/video: pin a Threat beat → watch the Pacing bar shift → open the Copilot panel → copy a "find holes" prompt.
3. Write landing copy around the pain ("You've opened Slide 1 a hundred times before you knew what the story was") with the three positioning lines as section headers.
4. Price at $29 launch / $39 regular; run pay-what-you-want ≥ $15 for the first 72 hours to seed reviews.
5. Post the GIF plus the Pacing-meter screenshot on LinkedIn and X — sales and founder audiences respond well to "here's the framework, here's the tool" posts.
6. Share in 3 relevant communities (Indie Hackers, r/sales, a sales-enablement or founder Slack/Discord) as a workflow story, not an ad — lead with the five-beat framework, mention the tool second.
7. Use it yourself to build the pitch for the tool's own landing page, and say so — it's a credible, low-effort proof point.

## Upsell paths

- **Done-with-you working session ($150–$300):** 60 minutes building the client's actual pitch arc live, beat by beat, ending with a Copilot-polished outline.
- **Category-specific demo boards ($9–$19):** pre-built arcs tuned to a vertical (SaaS renewal pitch, agency new-business pitch, fundraising pitch) that buyers load in place of the default demo.
- **Niche editions:** "Narrative Deck Builder for Founders Raising a Seed Round" or "...for Agency New-Business Pitches" — same engine, retuned demo data, hint copy, and Copilot prompt framing.
- **Team rollout package:** a short internal training doc plus a shared "house style" demo board other reps clone from, sold as a team license add-on.
