# Objection Dojo — How to Sell It

## Who buys it

Founders doing founder-led sales, solo consultants, and early AEs/SDRs who lose winnable deals because they fumble the same five objections every week. The pain is visceral and recent — they can name the exact call where "we don't have budget" beat them. Sales managers ramping new reps are the secondary buyer (one purchase per rep).

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Anki for sales objections — drill your counters until they're reflexes, not improv."
2. "Stop losing deals to the same objection twice. Build the counter once, drill it forever."
3. "A private sparring dojo for your sales calls — with Claude as your sensei."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39.** It's a focused single-purpose tool with durable value; under $40 is an impulse buy for anyone who sells. $29 is the sweet spot.
- Optional tiers: **$19** app only · **$39** app + a starter library of 60 pre-written objections/counters for common niches (agencies, SaaS, consulting) · **$79** team pack (5 licenses + shared-library JSON workflow).
- Rationale: the pre-written objection packs cost you a few hours with Claude to produce and multiply perceived value far beyond the code.

## Where to sell (free to start)

- **Gumroad** — best discovery for sales/marketing tools; 10% flat fee, no monthly cost.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, handles VAT if you sell to the EU.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you already post sales content.
- List the same product on all three; link them from one landing page.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo, take 4 screenshots: the heat/belt status strip, a library card, drill mode mid-reveal, and the Copilot panel.
2. Record a 30-second GIF: press D, reveal a counter, grade it, show the belt/heat update.
3. Write the landing page around one line: "You already know the objection is coming. Why are you still improvising?" Then the 3-step loop: capture, drill, spar with Claude.
4. Produce one free lead magnet with the app itself: "The 12 objections that kill consulting deals (and drilled counters for each)" — exported straight from the demo library as Markdown/PDF.
5. Post the GIF + lead magnet on LinkedIn and r/sales / Indie Hackers; the drill mechanic demos extremely well in motion.
6. DM 10 sales coaches and offer a free copy in exchange for a testimonial or a share to their audience.
7. Add a launch discount code (e.g. WHITEBELT, 30% off, 7 days) to create urgency.

## Upsell paths

- **Niche objection packs** ($9–19 each): pre-built JSON libraries per vertical — agencies, SaaS founders, freelancers, insurance, recruiting.
- **Setup service** ($99): a 45-minute call where you build the buyer's library with them using the Copilot prompts.
- **Team edition**: shared master-library JSON + onboarding doc for sales managers ramping reps.
- **Bundle** with sibling BD apps (Battlecard Bay, Discovery Call Copilot) as a "Call Confidence Kit."
