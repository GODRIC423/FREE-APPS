# Monetizing ICP Architect

## Who buys it

Solo consultants, boutique agencies, fractional sales/BD leads, and early-stage founders doing founder-led sales. Their pain: they "know" their ideal customer in their head, so every list they build is 40% wishful thinking — outreach hours burn on companies that were never going to buy, and nobody can say *why* a prospect made the list. This gives them a defensible, weighted definition and a ranked list before the first email goes out.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Stop guessing who to sell to — draft your ICP like a working drawing and score every prospect against it."
2. "The qualification tool for people who don't have a RevOps team: weighted fit scores, ruthless disqualifiers, zero SaaS bill."
3. "Your $20 Claude subscription just became a market research department — this is the workbench that aims it."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39.** It is a single-file tool with no hosting cost to you and no subscription fatigue for the buyer; $29 is a comfortable impulse-buy midpoint for a tool that saves one wasted outreach week.
- Optional tiers: **$19** app only · **$39** app + "ICP Sprint" PDF worksheet (a 90-minute agenda for running the first drafting session) · **$79** five-seat team license.
- Bundle candidate: pair with a cold-outreach or lead-list app from the same suite at $49 ("Aim, then fire").

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest start, discover feed, pay-what-you-want option for launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout and EU VAT handled if that's your audience.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you already have an audience there.
- Your own site later; start where checkout already exists.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo, take 4–5 crisp screenshots (drafting table, ranked survey, score drawer, Copilot desk, print view).
2. Record a 30–45 second GIF/screencap: draft criterion → weight it → score a prospect → tier stamp lands.
3. Write the landing page around the pain ("your list is 40% wishful thinking"), not the features; include the three positioning lines as A/B headline tests.
4. Publish on Gumroad with the demo GIF as the cover; price at $29 with a $19 launch week.
5. Post a build-in-public thread (X/LinkedIn) showing a real ICP being drafted and torn apart by the Red-pen review prompt.
6. Submit to niche directories and communities: r/sales, r/Entrepreneur (rules permitting), Indie Hackers, BetaList-style newsletters, and sales-tool roundups.
7. Offer the first 10 buyers a free 20-minute "ICP drafting session" in exchange for testimonials.

## Upsell paths

- **Done-with-you setup:** a paid 90-minute workshop where you draft the buyer's first two sheets live ($150–$400).
- **Template packs:** pre-filled sheet libraries per niche (agencies, SaaS, professional services) sold at $9–$19 each.
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned variants ("ICP Architect for Recruiters") with tailored demo data and criteria banks.
- **The suite:** graduate buyers into the full 30-app BizDev bundle.
