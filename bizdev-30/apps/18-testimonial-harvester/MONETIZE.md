# Testimonial Harvester — Monetization Playbook

## Who buys it

Solo consultants, freelancers, boutique agencies, and coaches who know social proof closes deals but have it scattered across old emails, Slack threads, and screenshots — and who feel awkward asking. They'll pay for a system that makes the ask easy, keeps rights clean, and turns quotes into assets they actually deploy.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "Every happy client is a testimonial you haven't asked for yet — this is the system that asks."
2. "From 'thanks so much!' in your inbox to a framed quote on your pricing page — with the rights cleared."
3. "A CRM for social proof: pipeline the ask, clear the rights, hang the wall."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $29–$49.** It's a focused single-purpose tool that replaces a messy spreadsheet plus an awkward process; $29 is an impulse buy for anyone who bills $100+/hr, $49 is defensible with the script pack framed as included templates.
- Optional tiers: **$29 Personal** (the app) / **$59 Studio** (app + 20 extra ask-script variants + a "proof audit" checklist PDF) / **$149 Team** (up to 5 licenses + 30-min setup call).
- Bundle: sell alongside sibling BD apps (Case Study Factory, Cold Email Forge) as a "Proof & Pipeline" 3-pack at ~40% off combined price.

## Where to sell free-to-start

- **Gumroad** — fastest to launch, discover tab, pay-what-you-want option for launch week.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — cleaner checkout, EU VAT handled (merchant of record).
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — zero monthly fee alternatives; Ko-fi works well if you already have an audience there.
- Your own site later; start where checkout is free.

## Launch checklist

1. Load the demo data and take 4 screenshots: the hero + funnel, an expanded pipeline row with the rights checklist, the Proof Wall, and the Copilot panel with a copied prompt.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF/video: add a person → copy a personalized script → paste a quote → it appears framed on the wall.
3. Write the landing copy around the pain ("You have the praise. You just never asked for permission to use it.") with the three positioning lines as section headers.
4. Price at $29 launch / $39 regular; enable PPWYW ≥ $19 for the first 72 hours to seed reviews.
5. Post the GIF + one framed-quote screenshot on LinkedIn and X; write one short post: "I built a CRM for testimonials, here's the system."
6. Share in 3 communities where consultants gather (Indie Hackers, r/freelance, a relevant Slack/Discord) as a "here's my system + tool" story, not an ad.
7. Eat your own dog food: use the app to collect testimonials about the app; put them on the sales page within two weeks.

## Upsell paths

- **Done-with-you setup ($150–$300):** 60-minute session — build their shortlist, personalize the four scripts, define placements.
- **Template packs ($9–$19):** industry-specific ask scripts (SaaS, agencies, coaches, trades) and extra frame styles.
- **Niche editions:** "Testimonial Harvester for Wedding Vendors / Real Estate / B2B SaaS" — same engine, tuned demo data, scripts, and placements.
- **Quarterly "proof audit" service:** review their wall and placements, deliver a gap report (the Copilot coverage prompt is your worksheet).

## License note

Buyer receives the single self-contained HTML file plus source. Suggested terms: single-user license — use on any of your own devices and client work outputs (screenshots, printed walls) are unrestricted; no resale or redistribution of the app itself. Offer a team license (5 seats) at ~3x the single price. Be honest in the listing: it's a local, no-account tool — their data never leaves their browser.
