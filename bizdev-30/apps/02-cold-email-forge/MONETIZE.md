# Cold Email Forge — How to Sell It

## Who buys it

Solo founders, freelancers, agency owners, and first-SDR hires who send their own cold outreach and can't justify $99/mo outbound suites. Their pain: they write emails in Google Docs, have no idea why replies don't come, and every "email checker" online wants a subscription and their prospect data. This is a one-time-purchase, offline tool that scores their copy honestly and pairs with the Claude subscription they already have.

## Positioning (pick one)

1. "The cold-email editor that tells you the truth before your prospect deletes you."
2. "Write, lint, and A/B your outbound sequence in one file — no subscription, no data leaves your machine."
3. "A $20 Claude subscription plus this forge beats a $99/mo outbound suite for sequence writing."

## Suggested pricing

- **One-time: $19–$39.** It replaces a recurring copy-assistant subscription for the specific job of sequence writing; under $40 is an impulse buy for anyone who sends outbound. Start at $29 with a $19 launch price.
- Optional tiers: **$29 Personal** (the app + guide) / **$49 Pro** (adds a niche prompt pack: SaaS, agencies, recruiting, local services) / **$79 Team** (5-seat license + white-label header).
- Bundle candidate: sell with a cadence/follow-up app as an "Outbound Duo" at $49.

## Where to sell (free to start)

- **Gumroad** — zero monthly fee, takes a cut per sale; best discovery for indie tools.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — merchant of record (handles VAT), no monthly fee.
- **Payhip** or **Ko-fi Shop** — no monthly fee, simple digital delivery.
- Your own landing page + any of the above as checkout.

## Launch checklist

1. Capture 4–5 screenshots: hero with the temper dial, a step scoring BRITTLE with spam flags visible, the same step rewritten to TEMPERED, the Quench Test preview, the Copilot prompt modal.
2. Record a 30–45s GIF/video: load demo → break a step with spammy copy → watch the meters drop → fix it → copy a Claude prompt.
3. Write the landing page around the "delete test" hook; lead with the before/after screenshot pair.
4. Ship the demo sequence inside (already done) so the product sells itself on open.
5. Post the before/after GIF to LinkedIn and X; write one teardown post ("I linted 5 real cold emails I received") linking to the product.
6. Submit to marketplaces and directories: Product Hunt, Indie Hackers launch thread, r/sales and r/Entrepreneur (follow self-promo rules), cold-email communities.
7. Offer the first 20 buyers a personal sequence critique in exchange for testimonials.

## Upsell paths

- **Setup + first-sequence service:** $150–$300 to write and temper their first sequence with them on a call.
- **Niche prompt packs:** industry-specific Copilot prompt and demo-sequence packs ($9–$19 each).
- **Niche editions:** re-skinned builds ("Recruiter Email Forge", "Agency Outreach Forge") with tuned lint dictionaries.
- **Team workshop:** a 90-minute "pass the delete test" training using the app as the exercise environment.

## License note

Buyer receives the single self-contained HTML file plus source, for use by **one person** (suggest a simple single-user license; team tier covers 5 seats). No resale or redistribution of the file itself. Be explicit that the tool stores data locally and ships with no warranty of deliverability results — it improves copy, it doesn't promise replies.
