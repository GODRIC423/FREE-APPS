    How to use Lead Leak Radar

    1. Set the business profile. Name, type, close rate (% of answered leads you win) and recovery rate (% of leaked value a fix can realistically win back). Every dollar figure derives from these.
    2. Add a channel for every place leads arrive: missed calls, website forms, shared inbox, booking flow, sent quotes, social DMs. Or press Load demo for a worked HVAC example.
    3. Estimate each channel: weekly inquiries, leak % (share that never gets a real response), average response delay in hours, average job value, and your confidence in those numbers.
    4. Read the radar. Blips closer to the center are hotter leaks; blip size shows the monthly $ leak. The stat strip totals lost leads, monthly $ leak and recoverable value live.
    5. Work the prioritized fix plan. It ranks channels by confidence-weighted recoverable dollars per unit of effort and flags quick wins — start at rank 1.
    6. Write the owner proof requirement so every fix ships with a success metric the owner will actually accept.
    7. Export the radar report: copy Markdown, download JSON (re-importable) or CSV, or print a clean report for the owner meeting.
    8. Safety nets: deleting a channel shows a 7-second Undo toast; Reset asks first; everything autosaves to this browser only.
