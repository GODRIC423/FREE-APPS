              How to use the Meeting Follow-up Kit

              1. Create a meeting. Click + New in the Meetings panel — or Load demo to explore with realistic data first.
              2. Fill in the details. Title, date, attendees, and your name feed the recap header and email signature.
              3. Paste raw notes. One thought per line works best. Click Scan notes for items and the kit suggests decisions, actions, risks, and questions you can add with one click.
              4. Capture structure directly. Use the quick-add boxes to log decisions, risks & blockers, and open questions as they come up.
              5. Track actions. Add action items with an owner, due date, and status. Overdue items are flagged automatically; update status inline as work progresses.
              6. Pick an email tone. Friendly, Professional, or Direct — the follow-up draft rewrites instantly. Copy it into your mail client when you are happy with it.
              7. Export the packet. Copy the Markdown recap, download JSON (re-importable) or an actions CSV, or print the recap page.
