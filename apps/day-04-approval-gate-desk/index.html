      How to use Approval Gate Desk
      
        
      
      
      
        
        
      1. Load the demo. Click Load demo in the header to fill the desk with a realistic queue: eight AI-drafted messages plus a started decision log.
        2. 
        
      2. Set your reviewer name at the top of the Review pad — every decision is stamped with it in the audit log.
        3. 
        
      3. Pick a draft from the queue. Status pills, the risk dropdown, and search narrow the list; high-risk pending drafts sort to the top automatically.
        4. 
        
      4. Read the risk flags under the message. The desk scans for guarantees, pricing promises, pressure tactics, legal exposure, and personal data. Override the level manually if you disagree.
        5. 
        
      5. Edit inline before approving. The original AI text is preserved; approving a changed draft is logged as "Approved with edits", and Revert to original undoes your edits.
        6. 
        
      6. Decide. Approve, request revision, or reject. Rejections require a reason from the taxonomy (overpromise, pricing, tone, factual, privacy, policy, wrong context, other) plus an optional note.
        7. 
        
      7. Audit the decision log. It is append-only, timestamped, and hash-chained — if the stored data is edited outside the app, the chain badge flips to "broken".
        8. 
        
      8. Export & handoff. Copy the Markdown packet, download a JSON backup or the audit CSV, or print a clean report. Everything stays a draft for a human to send.
