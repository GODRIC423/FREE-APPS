          How to author a field manual

          1. Draft a manual. Hit + Draft manual on the shelf (left), or Rack specimen manuals to study three finished procedures first.
          2. Fill §1 Identification. Title, owning role (a role, not a person), the trigger that opens the manual, cadence, purpose, what must be on hand, and a definition of done.
          3. Number the work in §2 Procedure. Each step gets a name, the exact action in plain words, and margin notes — owner, tool, minutes. Drag the grip or use the arrows to reorder.
          4. Write §3 Inspection. The short list an inspector checks before a run counts. Stamp a point here once you have verified it in a dry run.
          5. Map §4 In case of. For each way the happy path breaks: what happened, what to do instead, and who to escalate to.
          6. Watch §5 Certification. The seal inks in as the manual gets written; the Fix next list names exactly what is still missing.
          7. Issue & file in §6. Copy Markdown, print the issued page, download the shelf as JSON (re-importable), or the step table as CSV. Every copy is a draft for human review.
