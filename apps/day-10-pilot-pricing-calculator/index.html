    How to use this calculator

    1. Load the demo. Press Load demo in the header to see a fully worked missed-call recovery pilot, then swap in your own numbers.
    2. Describe the pilot. Name the client and the specific workflow being fixed — a narrow workflow prices better than a vague one.
    3. Build the value model. Enter monthly leads, the leak rate, average job value, expected recovery rate, and the proof window length.
    4. Enter delivery effort. Setup hours, weekly ops hours, and your loaded hourly rate set the walk-away floor — the price below which the pilot loses you money.
    5. Tune the sensitivity levers. Capture share and value horizon set the value anchor; risk, proof clarity, and strategic fit adjust it. Each lever shows its live effect.
    6. Read the formula breakdown. Every step from leads to final price is shown, plus deal-health flags (weak buyer ROI, floor above anchor, oversized scope).
    7. Compare packaging. Flat pilot, monthly, and performance-based options are computed from the same range — pick the shape that fits the buyer.
    8. Export the memo. Copy Markdown, download JSON/CSV, or print. Always human-review before sharing with a prospect.
