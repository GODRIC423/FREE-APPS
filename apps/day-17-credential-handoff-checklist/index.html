What belongs here

System name, owner, backup owner, storage-item label, MFA status, rotation date, revocation path, and human approval notes.

What never belongs here

Raw passwords, tokens, API keys, cookies, private keys, MFA seed phrases, recovery codes, customer PII, or payment data.

Safety boundary: This static local app creates a metadata checklist only. It does not encrypt, store, reveal, validate, transmit, rotate, or revoke actual credentials. Real secret exchange must happen in a dedicated encrypted/password-manager workflow.
