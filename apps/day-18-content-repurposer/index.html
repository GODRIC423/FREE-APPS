    How to use Content Repurposer
    
    
    Turn one long-form piece into a full publishing packet — every output stays a draft until a human approves it.
    
    
      
      
    1. Paste your source. Drop a transcript, build log, or article into Source content, set the type, audience, tone, and runtime. Blank lines between sections help the chapter builder.
      2. 
      
    2. Add takeaway, proof, and caveats. The takeaway seeds titles and hooks; proof notes are what the claim-check lint verifies stats against; caveats keep drafts honest.
      3. 
      
    3. Generate all drafts. One click fills every platform card: three YouTube title options, description, short post, LinkedIn post, and newsletter subject + blurb.
      4. 
      
    4. Edit inside the limits. Every card has a live character counter against the real platform limit — amber near the limit, red over it. Pick your primary YouTube title with the radio buttons.
      5. 
      
    5. Build chapters. Use Auto-suggest from source, then adjust timestamps (mm:ss) and labels. The builder validates the 00:00 start, ascending order, and the three-chapter minimum.
      6. 
      
    6. Clear the lint. The claim-check panel flags superlatives, absolutes, bait wording, and any stat that has no matching proof note. Resolve blockers, reconsider warnings.
      7. 
      
    7. Export the packet. Copy the Markdown, download .md or JSON, or print. The approval boundary is stamped into every export.
