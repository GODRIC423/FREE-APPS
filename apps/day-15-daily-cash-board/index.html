How to use Daily Cash Board

    
    
      
  1. Load the demo (header button) to see a realistic day: offers, follow-ups, a booked call, invoices, and one overdue collection.
    2. 
      
  2. Add your own cash moves with + Add cash move. Each move gets a lane, a dollar value, a confidence %, a due date, and one concrete next action.
    3. 
      
  3. Watch the stat strip: cash-in potential (value × confidence), cash at risk (overdue value), due-today count, and what you closed today.
    4. 
      
  4. Pick today's top 3 with the ★ button on any card. The focus panel keeps them in front of you; it resets each morning.
    5. 
      
  5. Work the lanes: Advance pushes a move to the next lane, Done closes it (counts toward "Closed today"), overdue cards glow red so risks never hide.
    6. 
      
  6. Filter with the chips (Due today / Overdue / Done) when the board gets busy.
    7. 
      
  7. End the day: the Export panel builds an end-of-day summary — copy it as Markdown, download JSON/CSV, or print it.
    8. 
      
  8. Everything is draft-only. No emails, invoices, payments, or CRM writes happen here — a human executes approved actions elsewhere.
    9.
