    How to use Local Biz Snapshot

    1. Create a prospect. Click + New (or press N) and name the local business you want to research.
    2. Fill the profile from what you can see publicly or were told directly — this app never scrapes; you are the researcher.
    3. Check leak signals you actually observed. Each checked signal raises that leak’s likelihood; add an evidence note for anything specific (dates, quotes, screenshots you took).
    4. Set the three fit sliders — revenue upside, proof visibility, and access ease. The fit score combines your sliders with the leak evidence.
    5. Review the recommended wedge. It targets your strongest-evidence leak. Keep it on auto or override it, then write why and the single human next step.
    6. Repeat for more prospects, then open the Compare tab to rank them by fit score and spot the best first call.
    7. Export. Copy the dossier as Markdown (Ctrl/Cmd+S), download JSON or the comparison CSV, or print — all drafts for human review before any outreach.
