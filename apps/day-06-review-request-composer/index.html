      How to use Review Request Composer
      
      
    
      
    
        
      
      1. Load the demo (header button) to see a realistic queue, or start clean with + Add. Reset any time from Export & handoff.
        2. 
      
      2. Fill the business profile in the left rail — business name, your name, and the review platform. Every draft uses these.
        3. 
      
      3. Pick a customer in the ask queue. Log the completed job, the completion date, and an honest satisfaction signal.
        4. 
      
      4. Add one specific job detail. A concrete detail ("finished early, cleaned up the crawlspace") is what keeps the ask from feeling like a blast.
        5. 
      
      5. Choose a tone preset and channel. SMS drafts stay short (segment counter included); email drafts get a subject line and a fuller body. Edit freely — Regenerate restores the template.
        6. 
      
      6. Check the pressure lint and timing advisor. Lint flags incentives, star-begging, gating, and guilt language. Timing tells you if the ask is too soon, in the prime window, or stale.
        7. 
      
      7. Copy the draft and send it yourself from your own phone or email. Then hit Mark asked so the queue tracks it — and Got review when it lands.
        8. 
      
      8. Export the packet — Markdown for a teammate, JSON for backup/import, CSV of the queue, or print it.
        9.
