How to use Quote Chase Board

  1. Add your outstanding quotes. Click + New quote (or press N) and fill in customer, value, win probability, and owner. Try Load demo first to see a working board.
  2. Set the stage. Every quote lives in one lane: Draft, Sent, Negotiating, Won, or Lost. Use the stage select or the quick buttons (Mark sent / won / lost).
  3. Watch the staleness badges. Each open quote shows days since the last touch — green under 4 days, amber from 4, red from 8. Click Log touch today whenever you actually contact the customer.
  4. Track follow-up dates. Set Next follow-up due; the board flags “due today” and “overdue” and the stat strip counts them.
  5. Draft the follow-up note. Select a quote, pick a tone (friendly, direct, or final nudge), and the drafter writes a note from the quote's stage, value, and age. Edit it, copy it, or save it into the quote's notes. Nothing is ever sent from this app.
  6. Read the stat strip. Open pipeline, weighted value, follow-ups due, and stale quotes update live as you work.
  7. Export your chase plan. Copy the Markdown plan (Ctrl/Cmd+S), download the board as CSV for a spreadsheet, or print a clean one-page plan.
  8. Back up or move your data. Download JSON for a full backup and import it on another machine. Reset clears the board.
