How to use Script Rehearsal Room

      
    
        
  1. Set the scene. Pick the call type, name the prospect, your single objective, and the promise you must not make.
    2. 
        
  2. Build the talk track. Each section holds short cards — a cue plus the exact line you'll say. Word counts convert to speaking time at 140 wpm.
    3. 
        
  3. Stock the objection deck. Add every piece of pushback you fear, with your best response on the back.
    4. 
        
  4. Drill flashcards. Flip a card to reveal your response, say it out loud, then grade yourself: Nailed it or Needs work. Shuffle to fight memorised order.
    5. 
        
  5. Rehearse against the clock. Set a target length, press Start, and speak the script. Pace hints tell you which section you should be wrapping.
    6. 
        
  6. Finish & score. End the run, rate Opening / Discovery / Objections / Close, and jot a note. Sessions build your trend history.
    7. 
        
  7. Export & hand off. Copy the Markdown run sheet, print it, or download JSON/CSV. Everything is a draft for human review.
    8.
