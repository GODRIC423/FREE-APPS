        How to use Warm Outreach Lab
        
      
    
        
    
          
      
        1. Add a prospect. Click + New prospect and give the local business a name. Each prospect gets its own research card, draft, and cadence.
          2. 
      
        2. Do the research first. Fill the research card: notes on what you found, one specific, checkable noticed issue, one honest proof point, and a low-pressure ask. Also list claims to avoid.
          3. 
      
        3. Compose the draft. Write in the draft box and click slot chips like {{noticed}} to insert personalization slots — they fill from the research card automatically in the preview.
          4. 
      
        4. Fix what the lint flags. The genericness lint scores the draft 0–100 and flags template-ish phrases, hype words, missing personalization, weak asks, and me-me-me writing. Aim for 70+.
          5. 
      
        5. Plan the cadence. Pick a follow-up template and start date, then apply it. Check off touches as a human completes them; due touches show in the stats.
          6. 
      
        6. Track the pipeline. Move status from Researching → Drafting → Ready → Sent → Replied. The stat strip shows send-ready drafts, due touches, and reply rate.
          7. 
      
        7. Export for human review. Copy the Markdown packet, print it, or download JSON/CSV. Nothing is ever sent by this app — a human reviews and sends.
