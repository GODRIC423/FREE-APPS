        How to use Technician Brief Builder

        1. Pick a job type and press Apply template. It seeds first checks, hazards, and a no-promise boundary — only into fields you left empty — and unlocks suggested parts.
        2. Fill the dispatch header: customer/site, contact & entry, technician, and service window.
        3. Describe the issue in the customer's own words, plus site context the tech should know before knocking.
        4. Build the parts & tools checklist — tap suggested chips or add your own, then check items off as the truck is packed.
        5. Review risk flags. The app scans your notes and suggests flags (access, electrical, promise pressure…). Add them with one click, adjust the mitigation, or flag your own.
        6. Complete the dispatch sign-off checkboxes and push the completeness meter into the green.
        7. Hand it off: print the one-page brief for the truck, or copy Markdown / download JSON from Export & handoff. Everything stays on this machine as a draft.
